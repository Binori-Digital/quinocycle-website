Nothing stalls a packing day faster than running out of tape at 10pm, or discovering your "big enough" box collection is three boxes short. Whether you're moving house or shipping 100 parcels a month for your online shop, the materials list is nearly identical — only the quantities change. Here's the complete checklist, with realistic quantities and Malaysian prices, so you buy everything once and get on with it.

## The Core Checklist: 6 Items, No More

Everything you genuinely need fits in one trolley:

1. **Carton boxes** — the right sizes, in the right count (details below).
2. **OPP tape, 48mm** — the standard brown or clear packing tape.
3. **Protective wrap** — bubble wrap, kraft/newsprint paper, or cloth you already own.
4. **Stretch film** — for bundling odd items and wrapping furniture drawers shut.
5. **Marker pens and labels** — one marker per person packing.
6. **Box cutter** — for opening, resizing, and flattening boxes later.

Everything else sold in the "moving supplies" aisle is either optional or a waste of money — we'll name names at the end.

## Tape: How Many Rolls You Actually Need

Tape maths that nobody tells you: a standard 48mm × 90-yard (about 82m) roll seals roughly **25–30 medium boxes** with one strip top and bottom. If you H-tape heavier boxes (a strip along the main seam plus one across each end seam), that drops to about 15–20 boxes per roll.

| Job | Boxes | Tape rolls (48mm × 90yd) |
|---|---|---|
| 2-bedroom apartment move | 30–45 | 3–4 |
| 3-bedroom house move | 50–70 | 4–6 |
| Online shop, 100 orders/month | 100–120 | 4–5 |

Buy one more roll than the table says. Leftover tape keeps for years; a midnight tape run does not.

> **Skip the dispenser regret:** a RM10–RM20 tape gun pays for itself within one packing day. Tearing tape by hand is slow, wastes 10–20% of the roll in stuck-together ends, and shreds your patience long before it shreds the tape.

## Protective Wrap: Bubble vs Paper vs Cloth

Three options, three budgets:

- **Bubble wrap** — best shock absorption for glass, ceramics, electronics. Costs the most and hogs storage space. Use it for genuine fragiles only.
- **Kraft/packing paper** — cheaper, moulds around odd shapes, doubles as void fill (scrunched into balls), and stacks flat. The default for online sellers.
- **Cloth you already own** — towels, t-shirts and bedsheets wrap plates and mugs for free during a house move. They had to be packed anyway; make them work for their ride.

For a house move, a practical mix is one small roll of bubble wrap for the display cabinet's contents, and cloth for everything else. For an ecommerce operation, paper usually wins on cost per parcel — it's also lighter, which matters once your parcels get weighed (see our [dimensional weight guide](/blog/ilmu-pembungkusan-berat-dimensi-0423/) for why grams and box size both hit your shipping bill).

## The Small Stuff: Film, Markers, Cutter

- **Stretch film** (pallet wrap): wraps drawer units shut, bundles curtain rods and mop handles, and keeps sofa cushions clean. One 500m hand roll covers a full house move.
- **Markers**: one per packer, thick tip, dark colour. Label the **side** of every box (room + contents) — stacked boxes hide their tops.
- **Box cutter**: for unboxing at the destination and flattening boxes afterwards. Retract the blade every single time you put it down.
- **Zip-lock bags**: for screws, cables and remote controls. Tape the bag to the furniture or appliance it belongs to.
- **For online shops**: add label pouches or A6 thermal labels for the airway bill, and tissue paper or stickers if you want a budget unboxing touch.

## What NOT to Waste Money On

- **"Special" wardrobe boxes for every closet.** One or two are handy for hanging clothes; beyond that, folded clothes travel fine in regular large boxes.
- **Foam peanuts.** Messy, static-clingy, and scrunched paper does the same job.
- **Printed "FRAGILE" tape.** A marker writes FRAGILE for free, and handlers respond to neither equally — good cushioning protects; tape only decorates.
- **Retail-priced boxes.** Hardware shops charge RM8–RM12 for the same box a warehouse sells for RM1–RM5. Box quality matters — see our [flute types guide](/blog/carton-box-flute-types-guide-0413/) for what actually makes a box strong — but retail markup buys you zero extra strength.
- **More than 3 box sizes (for sellers).** Every extra size complicates packing, storage and bulk pricing.
