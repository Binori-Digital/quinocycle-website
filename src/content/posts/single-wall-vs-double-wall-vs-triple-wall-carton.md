## What "Wall" Actually Means

A corrugated carton is a sandwich. Flat paper liners on the outside, a wavy fluted layer (the arches that give cardboard its strength) glued in between.

- **Single wall** = 1 fluted layer + 2 liners = 3 sheets of paper. Sometimes called 3-ply or "1 lapis".
- **Double wall** = 2 fluted layers + 3 liners = 5 sheets. Also called 5-ply or "2 lapis".
- **Triple wall** = 3 fluted layers + 4 liners = 7 sheets. Industrial territory.

Each extra wall adds crush resistance, stacking strength and puncture resistance — plus weight and cost. The shape of those wavy arches (flute types A, B, C, E) is a separate topic that also affects strength and cushioning; we've covered it in detail in our [guide to carton flute types](/blog/carton-box-flute-types-guide-0413/), so here we'll stay focused on the number of walls.

## Single Wall Cartons: Light, Cheap, Everywhere

Single wall is the workhorse of Malaysian e-commerce. Every Shopee and TikTok Shop parcel you've ever received was almost certainly single wall.

**Pros:**

- **Cheapest option.** Our new [A4 single wall box](https://shop.quinocycle.com) (310 × 220 × 220 mm) is RM3.00; the mini XS is just RM1.10. Used single wall starts even lower.
- **Light.** Less paper means lower parcel weight — and with couriers charging by weight, a lighter box can genuinely trim your shipping bill.
- **Easy to fold, cut and tape.** Faster packing at scale.
- **Compact to store.** Flat-packed single wall takes noticeably less shelf space than double wall.

**Cons:**

- **Limited load.** Around 10–15kg is the realistic ceiling. Beyond that, corners crush and the base sags.
- **Weak in stacks.** The bottom box in a tall stack takes the full load. Single wall at the bottom of a lorry stack is how plates get broken.
- **Humidity hits harder.** Malaysian moisture softens cardboard; a thin wall has less strength to spare.

**Best for:** apparel, books in small boxes, toys, packaged food, most online orders under 10kg, single-trip parcels.

## Double Wall Cartons: The Moving-Day Favourite

Add a second corrugated layer and the box changes character completely. Double wall is what we recommend by default for house moves and fragile goods.

**Pros:**

- **Roughly double the stacking strength.** Two flute layers share the crush load, so boxes at the bottom of a stack keep their shape.
- **Better cushioning.** The extra layer absorbs knocks — crucial for plates, glassware and electronics.
- **Survives multiple trips.** Double wall cartons are the ones worth keeping and reusing after a move.
- **Handles 20–30kg comfortably** when packed sensibly.

**Cons:**

- **Costs more.** Our medium [A3 double wall box](/shop/) (440 × 310 × 230 mm) is RM5.00 versus RM3.00 for the single wall A4 — and the large NLN double wall (590 × 390 × 390 mm), our moving-day bestseller, is RM8.00.
- **Heavier.** More paper per box adds up over a 60-box move — and over a courier invoice.
- **Bulkier to store** flat-packed.

**Best for:** kitchenware, glassware, electronics, house and office moves, boxes that will be stacked three high or more, long-term storage.

## Triple Wall: Almost Nobody Needs It (And That's Fine)

Triple wall (7-ply) exists for industrial loads — machine parts, automotive components, export packaging that replaces wooden crates. It's stiff enough to build pallet-sized containers from.

For homes and SMEs in Malaysia, triple wall is usually wasted money: it's expensive, heavy, hard to fold and overkill for anything a person can lift. | Layers of paper | 3 (1 flute) | 5 (2 flutes) | 7 (3 flutes) |
| Practical load | ~10–15kg | ~20–30kg | 30kg+ |
| Stacking strength | Basic | Strong | Industrial |

## The Simple Decision Rule We Give Customers

After supplying boxes to thousands of Klang Valley households and businesses, our rule of thumb is:

1. **Under 10kg, one trip, nothing fragile?** Single wall. Don't pay for strength you won't use.
2. **Fragile contents, or boxes will be stacked?** Double wall, no debate. RM2 extra is cheaper than one broken plate.
3. **Heavy AND fragile (glassware, appliances)?** Double wall PLUS good internal padding — the wall protects against crushing, padding protects against shock.
4. **Mixed house move?** Mostly double wall for kitchen and stacking boxes, single wall for linens, clothes and soft toys. Check our [moving box packages](/carton-box/) for a ready-made mix.
5. **Over 30kg?** Stop — split the load. Two RM5.00 boxes beat one RM28.00 box and one hospital visit for a strained back.
