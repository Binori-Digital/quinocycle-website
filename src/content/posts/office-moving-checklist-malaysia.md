An office move fails in the details: a missing server cable, a client invoice sent to the old address, forty unlabelled boxes nobody can trace to a department. The fix is not working harder in the final week — it is starting eight weeks early with a countdown everyone can see. This checklist is built for Malaysian offices of roughly 10–50 staff, and it complements our [30-day home moving checklist](/blog/pindah-rumah-checklist-30-hari/) if some of your team are relocating homes at the same time.

## The 8-Week Office Moving Countdown at a Glance

Print this table, pin it beside the pantry, and assign one owner per row. An office move without a named person-in-charge per task is how deadlines slip.

| Weeks before move | Focus | Key tasks |
|---|---|---|
| 8–7 | Decide & budget | Confirm new premises, set budget, appoint move coordinator, give landlord notice |
| 6 | Book & notify | Compare 3 mover quotations, book date, start SSM/bank/vendor notifications |
| 5 | Purge | Shred expired documents, dispose old furniture and e-waste, archive old files |
| 4 | Order supplies | Order boxes, tape, labels; brief department heads on packing rules |
| 3 | Pack storage | Pack storeroom, archives and items not needed daily |
| 2 | Pack common areas | Pantry, meeting rooms, reception; confirm IT cutover plan |
| 1 | Pack desks | Each employee packs own desk to the per-desk rule; label everything |
| Move weekend | Execute | Move Friday evening or Saturday; unpack essentials before Monday 9am |

## Weeks 8–6: Lock the Big Decisions Early

The first fortnight is paperwork, not packing. Confirm your tenancy end date and reinstatement obligations — many Klang Valley office leases require you to return the unit to bare condition, which needs its own contractor and timeline.

Get three mover quotations and ask each one the same questions: do they charge by trip or by hour, is there a surcharge for stairs or after-hours lift booking, and are boxes included (usually not, and mover-supplied boxes cost far more than wholesale). Book your preferred weekend early — month-ends fill up fast.

Start the notification list now because some items have legal deadlines. A registered office address change must be lodged with SSM within 14 days of taking effect, so brief your company secretary before the move, not after. Then work through LHDN, banks, insurers, business licences, utility accounts (TNB, water, internet) and your local council.

## Week 5: Purge Before You Pack

Every kilogram you shred or dispose now is money saved on boxes and lorry space. Malaysian offices hoard paper — clear anything past its retention period (generally 7 years for accounting records) through a shredding service, and dispose of broken chairs, dead monitors and ancient printers via an e-waste collector rather than paying movers to carry junk.

A realistic target: reduce your storeroom volume by 20–30%. That is often the difference between one lorry trip and two.

## Week 4: Order Boxes and Set the Rules

Now translate headcount into cartons. The working formula for a typical office:

- **2–3 boxes per employee** for desk contents, stationery and personal items
- **1 document box per drawer** of active files — the [Document Box JPM](/shop/) (380 × 185 × 250 mm, RM3.50) is sized so a full box of files stays liftable at under 12 kg
- **10–15 boxes** for the pantry, reception and each meeting room
- **Add 10% spare** — moving day always finds more

A 20-staff office lands around 80–120 boxes. For desk items, [used cartons](/used-carton-boxes/) from RM0.90 do the same job as new ones at a fraction of the price; keep new double-wall boxes for fragile and heavy items. > **Tip:** Never let staff pack files into large cartons. A big box packed solid with paper can exceed 25 kg — the base tears, the files scatter, and someone hurts their back. Small document boxes only for paper; big boxes for light, bulky items.

## Weeks 3–2: Pack From the Edges Inward

Pack in order of how rarely things are used. Week 3 is for the storeroom, archive files, seasonal decorations and marketing materials. Week 2 covers meeting rooms, reception and the pantry — leave one kettle and a few mugs out until the final day, for morale.

This is also the week to finalise IT cutover: confirm the internet line at the new office is live and tested **before** move weekend, and schedule servers and workstations to be shut down last and restored first. Keep the IT plan simple at this stage — photograph cabling, back up everything, and let your IT person or vendor own the sequence. Packing monitors, PCs and servers safely deserves its own process beyond this checklist.

## Week 1: The Per-Desk Packing Rule

Do not let the office manager pack 30 desks alone. Issue each employee their boxes and one rule sheet:

1. Maximum 3 boxes per desk unless approved.
2. Every box labelled on the **side** with department, name and box number (e.g. "FINANCE — Aisyah — 2 of 3").
3. Personal valuables and house keys go home with the owner, not in the lorry.
4. Drawers emptied completely — movers will not transport furniture with loose contents.
5. Desks cleared by Thursday 6pm before a weekend move.

Colour-coding by department (coloured tape or markers) lets movers deliver boxes to the right zone at the new office without reading a single label closely.

## Move Weekend and the First Monday

Move Friday evening or Saturday morning so you have the full weekend as buffer. Load sequence: furniture first, then boxes by department, with the "open first" boxes — kettle, router, printer, reception phone — loaded last so they come off first.

On Sunday, walk the old office one final time: ceiling storage, behind doors, the top shelf of the server room. Hand back keys and photograph the emptied unit and meter readings for your deposit claim.

Before Monday 9am the bar is deliberately low: internet up, phones ringing, each department's boxes in its zone, and pantry running. Full unpacking can take the rest of the week — operations cannot.
